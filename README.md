# Credit Risk Management Analysis — Finnable Credit Pvt Ltd (NBFC)

MBA capstone project analyzing credit risk management practices at an NBFC, based on primary data from 73 borrowers. Findings were later developed into a peer-reviewed paper published in the *Journal of Advanced Research in Operational and Marketing Management* (2026).

# Business Problem

NBFCs face higher credit risk than traditional banks due to their exposure to underserved, often self-employed borrower segments. This study examines how one NBFC's credit assessment process — income verification, credit history checks, repayment monitoring — translates into actual borrower repayment behaviour and customer satisfaction, and identifies where the system could be strengthened.

 # Objectives

1. Understand the loan approval and credit assessment process
2. Analyze customers' repayment behaviour and EMI payment patterns
3. Identify factors responsible for delayed repayments
4. Evaluate customer satisfaction with credit risk management practices
5. Recommend measures to reduce loan defaults

# Methodology

- Design: Descriptive and correlational research
- Data: Primary data from a structured questionnaire (73 respondents, convenience sampling)
- Period: 2 months (May–June 2026)
- Tools: MS Excel, Correlation & Regression Analysis, Data Interpretation, Pivot tables, Data & Financial Analysis, Percentage analysis

# Key Findings

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Monthly income ↔ EMI repayment behaviour (Pearson r) | **0.891** (strong positive correlation) |
| Regression model fit (R²) | **0.931** — independent variables explain 93.1% of variation in repayment behaviour |
| Customers who never missed an EMI | **76.7%** |
| Customers rating loan process as transparent | Majority |
| Customer satisfaction & willingness to recommend | High |

**What drives outcomes:** Customer Satisfaction (β = 0.421, p < 0.001) and Loan Process Transparency (β = 0.665, p < 0.001) were the strongest statistically significant predictors — meaning transparency in the lending process matters more to repayment discipline than EMI affordability alone.

# Recommendations

- Introduce flexible repayment options and support for customers facing financial difficulty
- Promote digital payment methods (auto-debit reduces missed EMIs)
- Continue transparency in loan procedures and regular EMI reminders
- Strengthen income-stability checks and continuous repayment monitoring
- Design customized loan products for self-employed borrower segments
